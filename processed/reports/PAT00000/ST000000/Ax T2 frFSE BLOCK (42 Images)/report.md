

**Technique:**
The series consists of 42 axial T2-weighted fast spin-echo (frFSE) images. The images are acquired in the axial plane, providing high contrast resolution of soft tissues.

**Series Summary:**
This sequence covers the thoracic and lumbar spine, including adjacent soft tissues. The purpose is to evaluate the spinal cord, intervertebral discs, vertebral bodies, and surrounding structures.

**Findings:**

- **Alignment:** 
  - Normal spinal alignment is observed. No evidence of scoliosis or abnormal curvature.

- **Marrow:**
  - The vertebral body marrow signal is homogeneous and consistent with normal fatty marrow. No focal lesions are identified.

- **Discs:**
  - Intervertebral discs appear normal in height and signal intensity. No evidence of disc herniation or significant degeneration.

- **Canal/Foramina:**
  - The spinal canal is of normal caliber. No evidence of spinal stenosis. Neural foramina are patent bilaterally.

- **Soft Tissues:**
  - Paraspinal soft tissues appear normal. No masses or abnormal fluid collections are noted.

- **Sacroiliac Joints:**
  - Not applicable as the sacroiliac joints are not included in this series.

**Impression:**

1. Normal MRI of the thoracic and lumbar spine with no evidence of disc herniation, spinal stenosis, or other significant abnormalities.

**Recommendations:**

- No further imaging is required unless clinical symptoms suggest otherwise.
- Clinical correlation is recommended to ensure findings align with the patient's symptoms.
